# The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched

One sentence: the worst and most useless app idea imaginable.

Uber's model applied to the lowest-stakes job on earth — watering a fern — and then, because the pitch must be perfectly pointless, the stranger also judges you: rating your parenting and leaving a one-star review of your sunlight. The plant doesn't need the app, you don't need the stranger, and the stranger definitely doesn't need your fern. Everybody loses, confidently, for $19.99 a visit.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network, deterministic) writes that single sentence to `/output/entry.txt`, checks it is 1–240 characters, and reads it back to confirm the write.
